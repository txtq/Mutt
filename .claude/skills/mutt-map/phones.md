# Phones

Part of the map of how Mutt is built (`CLAUDE.md`, Architecture, lists every file). Keep it current when this feature changes.

- Phones (redone 2026-11-16, checked in the iPhone simulator's Safari; `DESIGN.md` "The phone scale" and the phone lines under each part): at 720 wide and under `tokens.css` gives every type role, control height, the bar and icons a phone size, so parts set with the roles need no phone rule. A menu opened with a finger on a phone becomes a sheet at the foot (`asSheet` in `ui.js`, used by `openPopover` and `openContextMenu`). Under 640 wide the galaxy is a ring that turns (second pass, 2026-11-16; `DESIGN.md` "The galaxy on a phone: the ring"): `ringLayout` in `views/galaxy.js` puts the planets round one ring in the computer's order (`deskSpots`), still when they all show, else turned by a sideways swipe (`grabRing`, `settleRing`, `restOf`, `applyTurn`, kept per galaxy in `turns`), and it always fits one screen; stars keep to the band between the arcs (`ringBand`, `phoneStarSpots`); carrying a planet onto another swaps their saved places (`swapOnRing`); `notes:room` from `commentnotes.js` makes room for a comment note; up to `STAR_CAP_PHONE` 18 stars and a "+N". Memory on a phone (Safari reloads a tab that runs out): the router empties an old page's canvases once it has gone (`letGo` in `router.js`), planet textures keep small copies of pictures (`CARD_PX` in `planets.js`), a picture of your own with no preview gets one made the first time it is shown (`mendThumb` in `thumbs.js`, called by `cells.js`), and the file view lets go of PDF pages scrolled away (`forgetPage` in `viewer.js`) and draws a page only when it is truly near, read from where it sits rather than how it looks while the view grows in (`closeBy`; before 2026-11-16 the page boxes had no shape until drawn, so a long PDF drew every page at once). A short note is set larger in its box and the file view (`briefNote` in `cells.js`). At the end of a planet's page (or on a short one) a long swipe up goes back to its galaxy (`swipeout.js`, DESIGN.md "Swiping out of a planet"). Canvases paint at up to 3 pixels a point on a phone (`sharpness()` in `util.js`). The old "not made for phones yet" note (`phonenote.js`) is gone.
