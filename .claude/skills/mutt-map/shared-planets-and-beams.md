# Shared planets and beaming

Part of the map of how Mutt is built (`CLAUDE.md`, Architecture, lists every file). Keep it current when this feature changes.

- A planet kept together (binary planets, 2026-11-16; `DESIGN.md` "Kept together and beaming"): one planet in two people's galaxies, both able to add, arrange, rename, share and make moons. `drawer_keepers` (drawer, user, since, the keeper's own x/y); `keeper_of` / `keeps` in `mutt/api.py`, honoured by `own_drawer`, `drawer_visible`, `writable_drawer`, `is_unlocked`, `restore_block`; each keeper's own files stay theirs (neither can change or take off the other's). The owner asks (`POST /api/drawers/<id>/keep` {with}: only someone who already shares it, `keep_candidates`; `DELETE` takes it back; `keep_asks`), or makes one with someone from the start (`POST /api/drawers` {title, with}: shared with them by name and the ask sent at once, at most `KEEP_WAITING` 3 waiting per person; front end `planetWith` in `keeping.js`, offered from their person sheet (`personBody` in `statusbar.js`, opened by the bar's name on their galaxy), their file's Save list, menus and file view (`withOwner`/`planetWithOwner` in `actions.js`, `collection.js`, `viewer.js`) and a beam's "Put on a planet" list in `beams.js`; every word a person reads says "shared planet"; checks at the foot of `tests/together.sh`, browser path `planet_with`), the other answers on their galaxy (`GET /api/keep` gives asks and `keep_news`, said once; `POST /api/keep/<drawer>` {answer: yes|wave}). `POST /api/drawers/<id>/leave` and account deletion run `part_ways`: both keep everything (the leaver gets a private planet of the same name, moons too, with their files and copies of the other's; the stayer gets copies of the leaver's; a transfer of ownership clears its code). A kept planet can't be deleted or split, autosort never picks it (`sorting.galaxy(skip_kept=True)`), it comes in `drawers_of` and `galaxy_rows` for both, with `keepers` / `keptWith` in `planet_json`, and `galaxy_links` joins the two (`kept`). Front end: `keeping.js` (the ask tip and news, run by `statusbar.js`), the Share panel's `keepBox` and "Leave planet…" in `views/planet.js`, the caption mark in `planets.js`, `keptBetween` in `views/galaxy.js` (drawn once in the universe). Your data.json: `keptTogetherWith`.
- Beaming out / beaming in (2026-11-16): `beams` (block, from, to, words ≤ 140, quiet, noted, answer kept/gone, copy) and `beam_stops`. `POST /api/blocks/<id>/beam` {to, words} (limits: `BEAM_WAITING` 12 per pair, 3 when quiet, `BEAM_DAY` 60; quiet when they never crossed paths, `crossed`; to someone who stopped you it is silently dropped), `DELETE /api/beams/<id>` (Undo), `GET /api/beams`, `POST /api/beams/<id>` {answer: keep|gone|noted, drawer?}, `POST .../undo`, `/api/beam-stops` (GET, POST {username}, DELETE /<username>), `/api/beam/people?q=` (everyone, up to `BEAM_LIST`: those you've crossed paths with first, then A to Z by name). A waiting file is readable by its receiver only (`readable_block(beamed=True)`; not saved or commented), never counted as theirs; Keep makes a copy with `copy_block` (shared with saving; it drops the sender's place, time and camera, and `edit`/`original`) carrying `meta.beamedBy` (words only to its owner, `public_meta`). Quiet ones go after `BEAM_QUIET_DAYS`. `canBeam` and `beamStops` come with `/api/me`, `beams` (how many wait) with your own `/api/galaxy`, and the notes for people you know with `/api/comments/new` (`beams`). A beam keeps the form it was sent in (`beams.form`: 'recipe' from a cookbook's card, 'document' from a desk or the editor, else read from the file, `beam_form`); `GET /api/beams` gives a recipe its `card` (`card_json`) and a document its `page`; while waiting, a card's dish photo is readable by its receiver (`readable_block`). `copy_block` → `copy_form` copies a document's `docs` row and `doc_images` (addresses rewritten) and a card's dish photo (`meta.dishOf`, no star of its own while its card is there, `DISH_KEPT`), for saving too. Front end: `beams.js` (fetched when wanted: `beamTo(block, {form})`, `flyLight` the light on its way, `beamPile` by the void with each beam coming in once per device (`mutt:beams-landed:<username>`), `openBeams` the box with its dust border (`dustBorder`, `dustFrame` in `uvweb.js`) showing a card (`recipeCard` in `cookbook.js`) or a page (`docPaper` in `docs.js`), `stoppedPeople` the account sheet's door), the journey (`beamJourney` and `Galaxy.journey`/`towards` in `views/galaxy.js`: out to the universe in place on your own galaxy, else by opening `/universe` and going back; `html.is-beaming` while it travels), "Beam to…" also in the cookbook's card menus and the document editor's •••, "Beam to…" in `blockItems` (`actions.js`), `beamsWaiting` in `views/galaxy.js`, beam notes in `commentnotes.js`, "Beamed by" in `viewer.js`. Your data.json: `beamedOut` (never their answer), `beamedIn`, `beamsStoppedFrom`. Checks: `tests/together.sh`.
