# Constellations

Part of the map of how Mutt is built (`CLAUDE.md`, Architecture, lists every file). Keep it current when this feature changes.

- Constellations (2026-11-16; `DESIGN.md` "Constellations"): a few of the owner's stars joined with a name. Server (`mutt/api.py`, under "constellations"): `constellations` (title, `dismissed` = the planet offer waved away) and `constellation_stars` (one row per star, a star in one at most; deleted stars keep their row so a restore brings them back; a trigger on `connections` takes a star out the moment it is put on any planet). `constellations_of` (sent with your own `/api/galaxy` only, never to visitors) shows those with two live stars and `tidy_constellations` ends those with fewer than two rows. Routes: `POST /api/constellations` {stars, title}, `PATCH …/<id>` {title, dismissed}, `POST …/<id>/stars` {block}, `DELETE …/<id>/stars/<block>`, `DELETE …/<id>`, `POST …/<id>/planet` {x, y} (a new public planet of its name with its stars in order). `CONSTELLATION_MAX` 12. The export puts its stars in `Stars/<name>/` and lists them in `Your data.json`. Front end: `constellations.js` (`Sky`: the figures, their wisps, joining, naming, the menu, the offer at the foot of the screen, the keyboard's "Join with another star"); `views/galaxy.js` lays each out as a small planet (`items` into `scatter`, a `shape` in `footprint`), lets it be carried by its name with its stars (`makeFigMovable`; its place `constellations.x`/`y`, saved through `PUT /api/galaxy/layout` as `sky:<id>`; `pieces`/`putAt` treat planets and constellations alike in `place` and `swapOnRing`), gives stars each other as drop targets (`targets(carried)`, `reach` in `drag.js`) and takes a star let go in the open sky (`onLoose`). Checks: the constellation lines at the foot of `tests/galaxy.sh`, one in `tests/smoke.sh` (the download), browser path `constellation`.
