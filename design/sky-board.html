A sky that moves

Round two. Your mix is loaded (river, a hint of light, fringed stars in mixed colours, old film grain, slow) with the three things you asked for:

Stars. Most are now small soft points that gather where the dust is. Only a handful flare, and each of those has its own size, tilt and arm lengths, so no two match.
Light. Instead of rainbow bands, the sky is treated as the surface of a dark liquid lit from above, as in your picture: a pale sheen on the folds, and colour only as a thin line where each highlight ends.
The universe. No stars. Four small effects to choose from, each tied to the galaxies themselves.
