# Comets

Part of the map of how Mutt is built (`CLAUDE.md`, Architecture, lists every file). Keep it current when this feature changes.

- Comets (2026-11-16; `DESIGN.md` "Comets"): reminders you can see, always called comets and always saying they remind you. A note becomes one only when its owner presses "Make it a comet": `cometRow` in `viewer.js` offers it in a note's view when its words name a day (the moment mode `comet`, `read_comet` in `mutt/modes.py`, from `mutt/when.py` `read`, careful on purpose: no time alone, no fractions, no "every Tuesday", no diary dates; birthdays yearly). Nothing offers it while you type (taken out of the add boxes and the note editor 2026-11-16 at the user's word: "janky"). Any file of yours has "Make it a comet…" in its menu (`blockItems` in `actions.js` → `cometFor` in `remind.js`: a comet opens its menu, else `askWhen`, the box with quick choices that starts from what `GET …/comet` read (`could`; a file's name via `words_of`), whose `{day, time}` `POST …/comet` takes; a day picked by hand has no `said` and is never moved by `follow_words`). `when.read` reads every way of saying when (weekend, the 20th, end of the month, in March, in 2 hours, a time alone…; `_day_of`). In a list, comets sit at the top, soonest first (`order`/`isComet` in `listmode.js`; `saveOrder` keeps their own places underneath). A comet pressed in the sky opens its note over the page (`openComet`), not on its planet. Done ticks the note off on every list planet it is on and Undo (`back`) unticks it; `planet_comets` gives `ticked` so an open list follows. In list mode `GET /api/drawers/<id>/comets?tz=` (`planet_comets`) says which of your items are comets (a mark at the row's end, `cometEl`) and which could be ("Make it a comet" in the item's menu); ticking ends a comet, unticking brings it back. Kept in `reminders` (one per note: day, time, said, yearly, tz, `at` and `ahead` in UTC, `made_at`, what was sent, `closed`, `done_at`); `follow_words` re-reads it when the note changes, `roll_yearly` moves a birthday on. One reminder, due at its time or 09:00 for a day alone, no heads-up since 2026-11-23 (`comet_times` gives `ahead` = `at`; `soon` is always false; `push_due` sends once, at `at`; the calendar file has one alarm). Done or ticked off on its list, a comet is listed by `GET /api/comets` with `done` for `COMET_BURN` (10 minutes) and then not at all, so the galaxy burns it up once (`burn` in `comets.js`; `burnt` keeps it to once a visit; `comet:done` from `dealWith` starts it at once). Routes (owner only): `GET /api/comets?tz=`, `GET /api/comets/due` (what the service worker shows: titled Mutt, Mutt's icon, what it is for, then "Comet · when"), `POST|GET|DELETE /api/blocks/<id>/comet`, `POST …/comet/(done|snooze|close|back)` (`back` is every Undo), `GET …/comet.ics`. Push: `mutt/webpush.py` signs an empty Web Push (VAPID on P-256 by hand, key in `DATA_DIR/push-key.json`; only Apple's, Google's, Mozilla's and Microsoft's push hosts) to `push_subs` (`GET /api/push/key`, `POST|DELETE /api/push`); `start_comets()` (started by `server.py`) sends every 30 s; `MUTT_PUSH=0` turns sending off (tests do). `public/sw.js` and `public/manifest.webmanifest` are served from the root (`root_file` in `mutt/app.py`); an iPhone gets pushes only from the Home Screen. Front end: `remind.js` (loaded with the page: the words, every action with its Undo, `cometRows` = the menu, asking for notifications in `tell()`, and `cometNotes`, the note under the clock with Done / Later on every page, `startComets` from `main.js`), `comets.js` (fetched by `views/galaxy.js` on your own galaxy once the page is up; placed by `place()` at the end of `layout()`, in the most open sky (`room`)): the path, the heat, the painter (one canvas per comet: print tails, then the crisp `streak` and `glint`), the tags (no line; never over the notes under the clock or its own streak) and the hit buttons. What a comet's file is comes as `kind` (`item` for a list item, with `list`), `thumb` and `closed` from `comet_json` / `comet_on` in `mutt/api.py`; `cometThing` in `remind.js` says how each is opened, `openComet` opens a list item's `cometCard` (the Comet card) and anything else over the sky; the comet note can always be closed. Checks: the comet lines at the foot of `tests/galaxy.sh`, browser path `comet`.
