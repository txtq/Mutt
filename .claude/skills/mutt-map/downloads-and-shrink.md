# Downloading as another kind, too-big files

Part of the map of how Mutt is built (`CLAUDE.md`, Architecture, lists every file). Keep it current when this feature changes.

- Downloading as another kind (`downloads.js`, small and loaded with the page: the Download menu and what each kind can become; `convert.js`, fetched when a conversion starts, does the work in the browser): pictures by canvas (WebP falls back to the converter in Safari; PDF via pdf-lib), notes through the documents exporters (`docexport.js`), PDFs through pdf.js, sound and video through ffmpeg.wasm in `convert-worker.js`. The ffmpeg core (0.12.10, single thread, about 30 MB) is fetched once by the server into `DATA_DIR/models` and checked by sha (`mutt/transcode.py`, served at `/api/convert/core.js|core.wasm`; `MUTT_CONVERT=0` turns it off, `MUTT_CONVERT_DIR` points at a local copy). Progress shows in the uploads toast.
- A file over the upload limit (`MAX_UPLOAD_MB` in `uploads.js`, matching `MUTT_MAX_UPLOAD_MB`) is made smaller in the browser before it would be turned away (2026-11-16; `DESIGN.md` "Too big, made smaller"): `canShrink` in `uploads.js` says which can be (still pictures; sound and video up to 1 GB), `makeSmaller` runs one at a time and fetches `shrink.js` (pictures drawn again on a canvas until they fit, a JPEG's Exif carried over and set upright; sound and video through the converter, `ffmpegJob` in `convert.js`, which now queues every converter job, downloads too). The upload item carries `squeezed` {from, to}, `final` (no Retry) and `stop`.
