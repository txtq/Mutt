# Comments on files

Part of the map of how Mutt is built (`CLAUDE.md`, Architecture, lists every file). Keep it current when this feature changes.

- Comments on files (`file_comments`, routes `GET/POST /api/blocks/<id>/comments`, `DELETE .../comments/<cid>`): anyone who can see a file (`readable_block`) can comment, its owner too; the owner can remove any comment, a writer can take theirs back. `commentCount` comes with every block (`BLOCK_COLUMNS`) and shows under a tile. Front end: `comments.js`, the bottom of the file view's side panel; on your own file it stays hidden until someone has commented. Separate from a document's margin comments (`doc_comments`). The owner is told of comments they haven't seen (`file_comments.seen`; set when they open the file's comments, answer, or wave the note away; `GET /api/comments/new`, `POST /api/comments/seen`) in small notes under the clock on their own galaxy (`commentnotes.js`, started by `statusbar.js`); pressing one opens the file on its planet at its comments (`showCommentsOf` in `comments.js`). Pressing the clock brings back the last three, seen or not (`GET /api/comments/new?past=1`, `togglePast` in `commentnotes.js`).
